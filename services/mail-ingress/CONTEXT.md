# Mail Ingress — Glossary

Ubiquitous language for the mail ingress context. Glossary only: no implementation detail, no spec.

## Inbound Email

A message a potential client sent to a firm's intake address, as it arrives from the mail provider and before the intake context makes anything of it.

## Email Boilerplate

Text appended to an Inbound Email by something other than the person writing it — a corporate mail gateway's confidentiality notice, a legal disclaimer, an antivirus stamp. It carries no intent from the potential client, and reading it as if it did distorts what the firm is told about the matter.

Named "Email" boilerplate to keep it distinct from boilerplate code.

## Quoted History

The copy of the earlier exchange a mail client stacks under a reply — the firm's previous question, and the rounds before it. Like Email Boilerplate it is text the potential client did not write on this round, but it is text they or the firm wrote *earlier*, so reading it as new intent makes a conversation look like it is repeating itself.

## Auto-Responder

An Inbound Email sent by a machine rather than a person — a vacation reply, a mailing-list posting. It is never answered, because answering one starts a loop between two robots.
